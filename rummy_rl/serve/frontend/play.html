    How to play

    • On your turn, take one card (from the draw pile or the top of the discard pile), then throw one card.
    • You win the hand when all 13 cards are in groups and one group is a run of 4 or more. The game declares for you automatically.
    • Run: 3 or more cards of one suit in a row, like 5♥ 6♥ 7♥. Ace can be low or high, but K A 2 doesn't count.
    • Set: 3 or 4 cards of the same rank, like 9♠ 9♦ 9♣.
    • When someone declares, the other player adds up their loose cards: A, 10, J, Q and K are 10, the rest are face value. With no run of 4 or more, every card counts.
    • Points are bad. Reach 201 and you lose the match.
